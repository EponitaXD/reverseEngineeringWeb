How to Reverse Engineer an Amazon Echo!

If you are here, that means you’re curious about reverse engineering and want an executable example! For our Senior Design project, our team documented the reverse engineering of a second generation Amazon Echo. Our team hopes this step by step guide will help you deep your hands into reverse engineering:

Materials:

  • 2nd Gen Amazon Echo Dot
  • Logic Analyzer
  • iFixit Pro Tech Toolkit
  • Microscope
  • Soldering/Desoldering supplies
  • Progarmm Adapter socket 3in1 EMMC BGA153/BGA169 BGA162/BGA186/BGA221 series

For reference, the following tools will be numbered as we go through the process.

Step 1: Disassembling the Amazon Echo

Use the jimmy (11) and metal spudger (10) to take off the rubber and plastic from the echo.

Remove the four screws using the magnetic driver (14).

Take back cap off of the device.

Remove the orange ribbon cable from PCB using the jimmy (11).

Now that the main PCB is detached, find the side with the metal cap and pry off using the metal spudger (10). Identify the chips using previous RE work. Use heat gun at 410 °C. Heat up the back of the main PCB and front. Add flux to the firmware chip. Heat up flux and chip. Use angled tweezers (6) to pull the chip up and off the PCB. Clean chip using alcohol and q-tips taking care not to leave cotton treads in the connectors.
